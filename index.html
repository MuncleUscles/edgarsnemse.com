Edgars Nemše

I'm the chief executive of the GenLayer Foundation and a co-founder of GenLayer, a network that resolves contracts and disputes between people, companies and AI agents, using a jury of different AI models.

Product builder at heart: I like to own a thing end to end, from deciding what to build to shipping it. Programming since I was 12, and over a decade at early-stage companies. Based in Portugal.

Experience

  1. 2026 to now

    Chief Executive Officer, GenLayer Foundation

    Appointed September 2026, ahead of the token and mainnet launch. My focus is adoption, getting teams to build real products on intelligent contracts, and moving the foundation's own work (grants, treasury, governance) onto GenLayer itself.

  2. 2023 to 2026

    Co-founder and Chief Product Officer, GenLayer Labs

    Co-authored the GenLayer whitepaper and led the product from first prototype to public testnet: the blockchain's execution environment, the developer tools and the consensus design. Built and ran the product and developer-experience teams.

  3. 2022

    Quantitative Researcher, Millburn Ridgefield

    Researched and built systematic crypto trading strategies for an $8 billion quantitative investment manager in New York, using social-sentiment and on-chain data.

  4. 2020 to 2023

    Co-founder and Chief Technology Officer, StakeHound

    Liquid staking protocol that reached $300 million staked across eight proof-of-stake blockchains. Designed the product and built the first version.

  5. 2018 to 2020

    Product Manager and Engineer, Radix DLT

    Early employee. Built the desktop wallet and the TypeScript library, and worked through the protocol's consensus design with the core team, experience I later brought to GenLayer. StakeHound started here as an internal product and was spun off.

  6. 2014 to 2017

    Founding Engineer, Edurio

    School survey platform used by more than 600 schools and several ministries of education. Built it from the ground up as the only engineer for the first year, then grew the team to six.

  7. 2010 to 2014

    Freelance developer

    Paid work from 16 through university: websites, interactive exhibits built on microcontrollers, sensors and cameras, cloud data pipelines.
