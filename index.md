# Edgars Nemše

Also written Edgars Nemse. Canonical page: https://edgarsnemse.com/
Last updated: 2026-11-16

Edgars Nemše is Chief Executive Officer of the GenLayer Foundation and a co-founder of GenLayer, a network that resolves contracts and disputes between people, companies and AI agents, using a jury of different AI models. Product builder at heart: he likes to own a thing end to end, from deciding what to build to shipping it. Programming since he was 12, and over a decade at early-stage companies. Based in Portugal.

## Experience

- 2026 to now: Chief Executive Officer, [GenLayer Foundation](https://genlayer.foundation/). Appointed September 2026, ahead of the token and mainnet launch. His focus is adoption, getting teams to build real products on intelligent contracts, and moving the foundation's own work (grants, treasury, governance) onto GenLayer itself.
- 2023 to 2026: Co-founder and Chief Product Officer, [GenLayer Labs](https://www.genlayerlabs.com/). Co-authored the GenLayer whitepaper and led the product from first prototype to public testnet: the blockchain's execution environment, the developer tools and the consensus design. Built and ran the product and developer-experience teams.
- 2022: Quantitative Researcher, [Millburn Ridgefield](https://millburn.com/). Researched and built systematic crypto trading strategies for an $8 billion quantitative investment manager in New York, using social-sentiment and on-chain data.
- 2020 to 2023: Co-founder and Chief Technology Officer, [StakeHound](https://stakehound.com/). Liquid staking protocol that reached $300 million staked across eight proof-of-stake blockchains. Designed the product and built the first version.
- 2018 to 2020: Product Manager and Engineer, [Radix DLT](https://www.radixdlt.com/). Early employee. Built the desktop wallet and the TypeScript library, and worked through the protocol's consensus design with the core team, experience he later brought to GenLayer. StakeHound started here as an internal product and was spun off.
- 2014 to 2017: Founding Engineer, [Edurio](https://home.edurio.com/). School survey platform used by more than 600 schools and several ministries of education. Built it from the ground up as the only engineer for the first year, then grew the team to six.
- 2010 to 2014: Freelance developer. Paid work from 16 through university: websites, interactive exhibits built on microcontrollers, sensors and cameras, cloud data pipelines.
